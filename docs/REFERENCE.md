# Reference — Ford Mustang GT3

Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/ford-mustang-gt3-78c40bf430c94e588136b1412247bcbb

Notes taken from the model's page preview image: a front three-quarter view from a low camera angle. Only details directly visible in that image are recorded.

## Proportions

- Low, wide body with a long hood; cabin set back toward the rear.
- Short front overhang, with wide flared front fenders over the wheels.
- Raked windshield and low roofline; short rear deck with a raised rear wing visible behind the cabin.
- Large-diameter wheels relative to the body's overall height.

## Stance

- Very low ride height; the front splitter sits close to the ground.
- Wheels sit flush within the arches on low-profile tires.
- Wide front track with pronounced fender flares.

## Color

- Bright yellow body paint with black graphics.
- Black stripe along the hood leading up to the windshield; "MUSTANG GT3" in yellow on a black windshield banner.
- Black hood vents, black lower aero (splitter and intake), black rear wing.
- Yellow-and-black diagonal hazard stripes on the front corners and rear quarter panel.
- Driver's helmet visible through the glass: yellow top with a purple/blue lower section.
- Sponsor decals on the fenders (Michelin, Goodyear, Sparco, Multimatic).

## Grille

- Wide, blacked-out lower opening across the nose, split by a horizontal blade.
- Slim, angular headlight units flanking it, each with a cluster of small round LED elements.
- Blue oval Ford badge centered on the yellow nose above the grille.
- Narrow dark intake in each bumper corner.
